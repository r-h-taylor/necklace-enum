Necklace Enum

Necklace Enum lists the symmetrically distinct derivative superstructures of a parent lattice — the ways of decorating it with several kinds of atom — by generating each one directly instead of filtering out duplicates. Reading the sites of a superlattice column by column along the largest cyclic factor of its quotient group turns every decoration into a word on which translations act as rotations, so the distinct structures are Lyndon words and can be produced with a necklace algorithm. The Python package reproduces all published counts and is one to two orders of magnitude faster than earlier codes.
